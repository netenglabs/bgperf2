# Bounded reproducer minimization

Selected 2026-11-04 after completion of live staged prefix validation at
`82fc7c8`. This is phase 7 of the developer performance testing plan. Deliver
one usable workflow through implementation, public modeled walkthrough,
meaningful tests, independent review, required gates and local checkpoints.
No real build/worker, acquisition, source-history investigation or publication
is authorized by this implementation task. Preserve all existing evidence;
performance policy stays disabled and formal 10% calibration deferred.

## First supported scope

Minimize synthetic IPv4 BIRD/BIRD/sink recipes by changing selected peer count
and/or prefixes per peer. Keep engine, immutable role images, selected raw target
settings, pinning, completion contract and reproduction question fixed. Preserve
the original embedded source and recipe unchanged. Reduced candidates use a
declared canonical single-cell, per-peer source: selection/legacy identity,
ordinal/repetition/order/seed metadata are regenerated and disclosed. This does
not preserve arbitrary route subsets or introduce attributes, MRT reduction,
new daemon support, builds, target overrides or performance predicates.

Select one existing named assertion and preserve its observed `failed` status.
Candidate-dependent expected session/prefix counts regenerate; the assertion
name and capability remain fixed. A different failure or missing observation
does not preserve it. Results must be verified, recipe/target/image bound,
distinct originals with valid worker budgets and explicit owned cleanup.

## Search, accounting and evidence

Use a fresh original-recipe baseline, deterministic greedy coordinate proposals
(selected dimensions in declared order; floor, midpoint, unit decrement), then
a fresh final confirmation of the best observed recipe. Restart proposals after
an accepted reduction and never reuse a discovery observation as confirmation.
Passing points do not establish monotonicity or justify a global minimum.
Record every tested point and report bounded search limits.

The explicit finite trial budget includes the baseline, reserved/in-flight
attempts and final confirmation. Reserve a final slot before further search.
An unmeasured or operationally uncertain observation stops successors; baseline
failure absence and unsuccessful confirmation have distinct conclusions. A
confirmed reduction requires a smaller recipe and a fresh matching final
observation, not merely exhausted budget or a passing helper command.

Planning/status/reporting are offline. Explicit single-step execution reuses
the existing recipe runner; an explicit reservation/manual-result-record path
also supports returning verified originals. Journal before launch, never
automatically retry a pending attempt, and allow bookkeeping recovery only from
its already collected verified result. Preserve partial evidence and reject
duplicate original IDs. Portable reports bundle candidate recipes and result
packages, rehash and reconstruct the search independently, and verify after
relocation without runtime access.

## Implemented workflow and focused qualification

The pure contract/search, explicit session CLI, recovery and portable reports
are implemented. Candidate source projection retains immutable role references
and fixed runtime controls, and the fresh baseline additionally freezes all
three observed physical image IDs. Rehashed journal edits cannot change the
selected assertion's capability, expected value or deadline. Missing images,
wrong worker budgets, inconsistent clock anchors, incomplete cleanup and
unmeasured selected observations cannot establish preservation.

The maintained seven-case public CLI walkthrough passes with explicitly modeled
runtime boundaries. Its ordinary reduction and recovery cases shrink 1 peer ×
8 prefixes to 1 × 2, each using five distinct observations including fresh final
confirmation. The three-trial budget case preserves the original after its sole
candidate fails; it reports no reduction. Failed confirmation, unrelated failure,
unmeasured work and duplicate original IDs retain their explicit non-confirmed
or recovery-required meanings. Every case verifies, rebuilds, relocates and
rejects rehashed prose tampering without runtime access.

Focused checks passed 170 tests covering the core, sessions, CLI walkthrough and
documentation mappings, followed by one added public CLI case that reduces
4 peers × 8 prefixes to 1 × 1 and uses a distinct fresh confirmation. These
checks use actual package readers and modeled observations; they provide no
live minimizer, daemon-failure or performance qualification. Tests also cover
verified pending-package reporting without promotion, stored-package export
without its former store, copied-session mutation refusal and strict explicit
execution booleans.
